# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First NPZSee release, derived from and crediting LiukDiihMieu/npyquick.
- Native Rust desktop viewer, array sidebar and cancellable background loading.
- Read-only mapped NPY/NPZ storage, virtual tables and multidimensional navigation.
- Unicode, scalars, JSON trees and click-to-open docked cell inspection.
- Min/max envelope plots, zoom/pan, X/Y and complex views, filled histograms and SVG.
- English, Simplified Chinese, French, Spanish, Russian and Arabic interfaces.
- Embedded OFL fonts, remembered language choice and general-purpose product copy.
- Standalone archives and pip wheels for Windows, Linux and macOS, x86_64/ARM64.
- GPL v3-or-later, retained third-party notices and corresponding-source archives.

Platform build and validation results are tracked in docs/release-status.md.
